I'm a final-year dual-degree student at IIT Madras working on **quantitative finance**, **control and optimization**, and **machine learning**.

My research is on learning-based control for safe robotics. At the Cyber-physical Systems Group, Aalto University, I worked with Prof. Dominik Baumann on diffusion models as predictive controllers. That work became [TCA-DPC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19380), a method that adapts a pretrained diffusion controller to new safety constraints without retraining.

On the quant side I build research infrastructure and signals: a survivorship-bias-free S&P 500 membership database, portfolio construction with robust covariance estimation, about 100 researched equity alphas, and a C++ limit order book (in progress). I also won the Physical AI track at Toyota Hackcelerate 2026 and am a [Codeforces Specialist](https://codeforces.com/profile/baltaru).

I approach problems through optimization, probability and optimal control, and I write C++ and Python to make the results reproducible and measurable.

**I'm open to Quant Research/Dev, Robotics & Control, and ML roles (2027 graduate).** See my [projects](/projects/) or [résumé](/assets/pdf/Ojas_Phadake_Resume.pdf).
