    Test-Time Constraint-Aware Diffusion Predictive Control
    Braghadeesh Lakshminarayanan, Ojas Phadake, Qi Chen, and Dominik Baumann
    arXiv preprint arXiv:2611.19380, Nov 2026
    Abs arXiv Bib PDF Code

    Diffusion models can learn multimodal distributions over finite-horizon state-input sequences, providing a way to generate candidates for predictive control. However, sequences generated by a pretrained model may violate safety constraints introduced at deployment, for example when obstacles are added or repositioned. Retraining whenever these constraints change limits the usefulness of learned predictive controllers. In this paper, we propose Test-Time Constraint-Aware Diffusion Predictive Control (TCA-DPC), an algorithm for adapting the sampling distribution of a pretrained diffusion controller to newly introduced safety constraints without retraining. We treat the pretrained model as a prior over finite-horizon sequences and define an adapted distribution through exponential tilting based on a constraint-violation penalty. The tilt strength is adjusted online using the fraction of generated sequences that fail constraint verification. We approximately sample the adapted distribution using the Metropolis-adjusted Langevin algorithm in the Gaussian noise space of the pretrained diffusion sampler.
